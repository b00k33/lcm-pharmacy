2 · Something bigger the audit found

BeforeEvery copy, plan, cancel and Save mine said its “✓ done” line through phFlashShow. That repaints the whole page, and the patient profile has no place to show that line, so it waited and popped up later on some other page.
NowOne small pill at the bottom of the screen, nothing repainted. The button you tapped turns into a tick for a moment, then goes back, at the same width, so nothing beside it moves.
