Tap a tile to filter (a herb heading picks its whole family). Scores are from your screenshot; the body figure is a simplified stand-in drawn bigger.

Option A · Family cards

Each herb sits at the top of its own card and its formulas sit underneath on a branch. Herbs with no formula are one quiet row at the bottom.

What changed
  • Da Huang · Da Cheng Qi Tang · Yin Chen Hao Tang are one red card now. Same for Chai Hu with its five formulas, Huang Qi with two, and so on.
  • Tiles are one small height (26 px). A bigger score still means a wider tile, so Da Cheng Qi (28) is wider than Yin Chen Hao (5). The number and a thin fill bar sit inside; the full "28 of 146" shows on hover.
  • The figure is nearly twice as wide (200 px, was 112 px) and lights up the organs of whatever tile you point at.
  • Cards sort by the family's best score, so the family that leads is always first.

Option B · Linked rows

One row per herb family: the herb on the left, a line out to its formulas, a faint band of the herb's colour behind the whole row.

What changed
  • Reads like a family tree, top to bottom: herb, then its formulas. The band and the line are what say "these belong together".
  • Same small tiles and fill bars as A. Each row ends with the family's total.
  • Rows sort by the best score in the family. Herbs with no formula share one row at the bottom.
  • On a phone the formulas drop under the herb instead of beside it.
