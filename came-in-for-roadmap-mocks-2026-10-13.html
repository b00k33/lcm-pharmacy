Came in for · 3 roadmap mocks

All live: tap a name to edit, tap how long to change it, x to remove, type to add. Same data in each. No suggestion chips anywhere.

Option A · Vertical road

One line runs down the card. The main complaint is the big dot at the top; each secondary complaint hangs off the line below it. No column headings.

What it does
  • Reads top to bottom: Main, then Also. The size of the dot says which is which.
  • How long and the start date are one quiet value on the right (1 yr · ~Oct 2025); tap it for the quick picks and the exact-date box.
  • Plan tag stays next to the main complaint. "+ add another" is the last, dashed dot.
Same card at phone width

Option B · Two lines

The whole story in two lines: a Main line and an Also line. Secondary complaints are small pills in a row, so three complaints still take two lines in total.

What it does
  • Smallest on screen of the three; nothing to scan. Good when there are 1 to 3 secondary complaints.
  • Each pill is name · how long; tap the how long to change it (same quick picks), x removes it.
  • More than about four pills wrap onto a second Also line.
Same card at phone width

Option C · Timeline

A true roadmap in time: every complaint sits where it started, left = longest ago, right = today. Main is the big dot.

What it does
  • Shows the order things began in, which the other two do not. Complaints with no date yet wait on the right as "when?".
  • Editing happens in the small list under the line (same one-line rows), so the picture is only a picture.
  • Needs about 480px; on a phone it folds into Option A.
