Ask her Visit: Mock · desktop · weights are the app's real ones · tap answers, watch the top 3
How it works. A rail page under Today's visit (after Abdomen), never counted in "n of m done". It reads the top 3 families right now and asks the general questions each one leans on: each topic once (the basics, sleep, bowels, appetite and heat, are always there; the rest follow the families), with a coloured dot on every answer that supports one of the top 3. Every answer ticks the matching tends to… signs that already exist on the Ren list, so scores, Patterns and the Treatment table follow with nothing new to learn. As you answer, the top 3 can change and the questions change with it, but anything you have answered stays. Return visit: last answers come up pre-selected with the date.
