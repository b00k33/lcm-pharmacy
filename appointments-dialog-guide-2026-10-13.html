1Words you do not need. The long grey hint in the paste box, "Day, if the paste has no date headings", the sentence about herbs, "Or add one" and "Repeat" are all instructions. Your standing rule is no how-to captions.
2Newspaper columns. The window is the Medical history dialog reused, and that dialog flows its text into three columns. So "Booked ahead" starts in the middle of column 2 and carries on in column 3, and the paste box has nothing to do with the list beside it.
3Names cut short. Each booked row is squeezed into a third of the width, so names and details end in "…".
4Why it blinks. Tapping Add, ×, a day chip or the herbs switch rebuilds the whole dialog from nothing (the box you were typing in, the scroll position and the focus all reset). It then rebuilds the whole Appointments calendar behind it, and once more when the confirmation message shows.
5Mixed looks. Emoji (calendar, camera, leaf) beside plain "＋" and "×" characters, while the rest of the app uses its own icon set. Add and Home visit look the same, and the day chips, date box and Repeat menu are three different shapes.
