Pulse chart: normal, today and last visit in one chart

Each cell can hold three things: your normal pulse (the grey baseline), today's reading and, when there is one, last visit's reading. Below are four ways to show all three without mixing them up. Every option uses the same made-up right wrist: Up. Resp at Qi today is pounding (last visit normal), Stomach at Blood is wiry both times, Bladder at Organ was weak last visit and is blank today, Lungs at Qi is slight pounding today only, and so on. The colours are placeholders: today in the chart's green, last visit in blue, normal in grey.

Today, what the app does: today's ticks are green on a green cell; last visit's are small and faded with a dashed cell border; the normal pulse is also dashed. Two dashed things are what make it hard to read. Every option below drops the dashed border for last visit.
On a phone the cells are about 40px wide, so options B and C keep last visit small; A and D draw it full size.
