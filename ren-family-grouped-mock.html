What changes

The list on the right of the figure stops being one long run of 18 names. Each herb family gets a heading, its own row first, then the formulas built on it, indented. Families are ordered by their highest score, so the strongest still sits at the top.

Tap a heading and the figure shows every sign that herb or any of its formulas weighs. Tap one formula and it narrows to just that one. The pill beside the group chips says which, and Show all clears it. The bars, percentages and colours are the ones you have now.
