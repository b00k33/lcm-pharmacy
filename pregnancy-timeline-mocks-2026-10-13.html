Option A · Ruler and checklist

One week ruler (0 to 40, three trimesters) with a pin per item, and a real table underneath. The table is where you mark things done and note results.

How it works
  • Pins sit at the middle of each item's usual window. Filled = done, teal ring = in its window now, amber = window passed and not done, plain = coming up.
  • "Mark done" asks for the date and a short result ("NT 1.4 mm, low risk"). It stays on the timeline and in the table.
  • The bar's today marker comes from the pregnancy start date you already set in the Pregnancy block.

Option B · Lanes

Three lanes (Blood tests, Scans, Visits and jabs) on the same 0 to 40 week scale. Each item is a bar covering its usual window, so you can see what is due together.

How it works
  • Bars show the whole window, not one point, so "weeks 8 to 12" reads as 4 weeks wide.
  • Tap a bar and its card opens under the lanes with the date, result and links (open the Blood tests page, or the scan).
  • Best for seeing the blood test that should go with a scan on the same trip.

Same timeline on the other fertility plans

Natural fertility, IVF Protocol and Cycle & IVF Protocol get the same component on a date axis, with their own checklist. Switch between them here.

How it works
  • Same pins, same table, same "Mark done". The axis is dates (last 3 months to next 3) instead of weeks.
  • IVF items read from what you already enter on the IVF history (egg collection, transfer, scans), so nothing is typed twice. Blood items read from the Blood tests page.
