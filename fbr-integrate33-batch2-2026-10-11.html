3 · Side effect to know about

A sign takes the colour of the family that weighs it highest, so 22 older signs changed colour on the rows and the Ren figure because one of the new families rates them higher: e.g. Dark or purple tongue and dark clots (Chai Hu → Xue Fu Zhu Yu), Easily startled and navel pulsation (Gui Zhi → Chai Hu Jia Long Gu Mu Li), Belly pain and muscle spasms (Gui Zhi → Si Ni San), Irritability and period pain (Chai Hu → Xiao Yao San), Bitter taste and poor appetite (→ Xiao Chai Hu). Scores are unchanged; only the colour moved.
