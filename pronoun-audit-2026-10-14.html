What was wrong

The app has one helper that knows a patient's sex (she, he, or they when it is not set). Only a few screens used it. The rest had "her" typed in by hand, so a man's page said "In her words", "Her build", "Ask her" and more. I scanned every piece of text in the app (about 68,000 strings) and found 337 uses of she / her / hers.

Now

Man

"What brought him in?" · "In his words" · "His build" · "Ask him"

Woman

Exactly as before: "In her words" · "Her build" · "Ask her"

Sex not set

"They / their / them" ("Ask them"). Never guessed from the name.
