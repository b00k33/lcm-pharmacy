integrate33: Si Ni Tang (21st) and Zhen Wu Tang (22nd)

Both are built on Fu Zi and sit under it in the family list. Si Ni Tang is not Si Ni San (the Chai Hu one). Each of your Bing and Ren tables is the same list, so every line scores on both sides at your weight: a Bing sign for today and a Ren twin for the tendency. A line you wrote with "or" or "and" is one line, scored once. Each formula tops out at 81 (Si Ni Tang) and 82 (Zhen Wu Tang) on each side.

Both formulas contain prepared aconite (zhi fu zi). Your safety notes are kept with each formula in the app. Suspected shock, heart failure, severe dizziness, reduced urination or marked swelling needs medical assessment, so the app only ranks signs and never suggests the formula.
