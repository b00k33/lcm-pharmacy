# Xiao Jian Zhong Tang · indications (Bing) and constitution (Ren)

Source: her notes, pasted 2026-10-05. Ratings are her importance scores, out of 5.

## Telling it apart from Gui Zhi Tang
- **Gui Zhi Tang:** exterior deficiency with sweating and wind sensitivity.
- **Xiao Jian Zhong Tang:** deficiency + chronic spasmodic abdominal pain + muscular tension + weakness.
- The **abdominal pain is the central feature.**

## The most important constitutional distinction
The classic Xiao Jian Zhong patient is often: **thin + weak + easily fatigued + thin abdominal wall + tight rectus abdominis muscles.**

## Contraindications / when to avoid (her notes, 2026-10-05)
The formula is warm, sweet and tonifying, for deficiency. It is poorly suited to excess, heat and significant stagnation.

| Contraindication / caution | Why |
|---|---|
| Interior excess pattern | Designed for deficiency and weakness, not strong excess. |
| Strong internal heat | Its warming and tonifying nature can aggravate significant heat. |
| Red tongue with thick yellow coating | Suggests interior heat/excess, not the Xiao Jian Zhong pattern. |
| Stiff, tough tongue | Contrasts with the expected tender, soft tongue of deficiency. |
| Strong, forceful pulse | Suggests excess rather than deficiency. |
| Static blood / blood stasis predominance | Fixed, stabbing pain with stasis signs is not the classic indication. |
| Severe fixed abdominal pain | Especially with clear excess or stasis signs. The formula suits intermittent, spasmodic pain. |
| Marked abdominal fullness/distension from excess | Better for a thin, deficient patient with abdominal tension than a full, excess presentation. |
| Prominent damp/phlegm accumulation | The sweet, tonifying nature may be inappropriate when substantial accumulation is present. |
| Significant digestive stagnation | Heavy fullness, food accumulation, thick coating and strong pulse point away from the formula. |
| Strong constitution with excess abdominal presentation | Especially when the pain occurs with a robust rather than deficient presentation. |
