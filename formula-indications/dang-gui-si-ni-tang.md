## One-line memory aid
Dang Gui Si Ni Tang = very cold + painful/numb extremities + thin pulse + pale tongue/white coat, especially when pain is worse with cold.

**Distinction from Gui Zhi Tang:** Dang Gui Si Ni Tang moves further toward cold, vasoconstriction, poor peripheral circulation, numbness and pain.
