What this adds

  • Body card on Patient details: height, weight, body fat %, skeletal muscle kg. BMI, fat mass and muscle % of weight work themselves out.
  • Dated readings: Save reading keeps each set with its date, and the chart uses the latest. Trends show as ▲ ▼.
  • Drawn to scale: height sets the length, and BMI, fat and muscle set the width at the shoulders, waist, hips and thighs. The dashed outline is the average for her sex, so you see how she differs.
  • Fang Bing Ren: the numbers pre-tick the build signs (thin, heavy, soft and loose, robust). They are labelled "from measurements", and you can untick any of them like any pre-filled sign.

Across the app

  • Ren, Pain map, Musculoskeletal, Patterns and Fang Bing Ren all use the same sized figure.
  • One shared set of organs. Today Ren and Patterns put the uterus and liver about 30 units apart.
  • One nine-zone belly grid and one left and right rule. The abdomen chart and Ren use two different grids now.
  • The Ren-tick stretch (thin or heavy) goes. Real proportions replace it.
  • Tongue, pulse and cycle charts do not depend on size, so they stay as they are.

Calls to check

  • The ranges and sign rules are my draft from standard tables.
  • No numbers entered means the average figure and no pre-ticks, as today.
  • A sign you tick yourself always beats one from the numbers.
  • The warp is an estimate of shape, not a scan. It says so on the figure.
