2 · How the next question is chosen

1
Every family starts equal. Your 0 to 5 weight becomes "how likely is a Yes if this patient is that family": 5 is 90%, 3 is 58%, a sign the family does not list is 10% (anyone can have it), a minus weight is 5% (counts against).
2
Every answer multiplies. A Yes multiplies each family's chance by its own number for that sign. A No multiplies by the leftover. Opposites are auto-answered. Charts, history and cycle answers already in the app count as answers too (no question needed).
3
Pick the question that separates the leaders most (the biggest expected drop in uncertainty). It also tells you why, for example "Separates Gui Zhi (key sign 5) from Ma Huang (counts against)".
4
Rule families out as you go. Under 3% and a family is greyed out with the one answer that did it. It stops being asked about.
5
Stop when it is clear. At 85% for one family it says "Clear lead" and offers Use this or Keep asking. A Skip ("not sure") is remembered and never changes the numbers.
