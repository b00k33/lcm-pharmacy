Linked symptoms engine design + working demo

One answer can settle several others, in both directions, with a record of who answered what. This page checks your seven requirements against what the app does today, then lets you try the behaviours so you can pick the ones you want.

3 · How the links are written configurable

A link is one line, and it reads both ways by itself. "Side A" and "Side B" cannot both be Yes. Any number of signs can sit on either side, so one answer settles many (and you never type the reverse).

The 25 links already in the app move into this table unchanged. Each can be switched off, edited, or joined by new ones from a "Linked symptoms" page, and a ⋯ on any sign row says "Link to…". Built-in links are marked; yours are marked "yours".

  • Chains: when a link sets an answer, that answer's own links are checked too, with a loop guard and a limit of 4 steps, so nothing runs away.
  • Priority: your own answer beats a chart finding, which beats a link. A link never overrides your answer or a chart. It can only fill blanks, or raise a flag.
  • Unknown: a blank stays blank. A link only ever sets "No" on the signs it names, and never sets a "Yes".
