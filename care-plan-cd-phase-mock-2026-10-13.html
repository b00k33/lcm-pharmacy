Cycle day and the phase

Your screenshot (Sylvia's Care Plan, the Course side). Today the CD column shows a number and a ring, and the cycle phase only appears if you hover. The plan phases (Follicular, Ovulation, Luteal) never say which cycle days they cover. Three ways to make them correspond, drawn on the same screen: tap a phase row or a visit to see what lines up. CD windows here are the app's own for a 28-day cycle with a 5-day period.

What stays the same

  • The Session grid keeps its shape: the header row, one row per phase, squares, This week.
  • The CD itself, the ring and the filing of visits are unchanged. Only what is shown beside them changes.
  • Phases that are not on the cycle (Early Pregnancy) show no days.

What to look at

  • The app's windows make Week 2 one day (CD 13) in a 28-day cycle: period 1-5, Week 1 6-12, Week 2 13, ovulation 14-15, luteal 16-28.
  • Two visits do not fit the phase they fill: CD 15 is the ovulation window but fills Week 2, and CD 29 is past day 28 (period due) but fills Luteal.
  • Sylvia's real cycle length and period days may differ from this 28 / 5 sketch.
