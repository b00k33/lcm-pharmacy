3 · How it moves

Quiet, only when something changes

Logging a day fills its calendar square (a soft pop) and the sentence or status line updates in place. A newly estimated ovulation date fades in once. The gold dot beside "Pregnant?" stays still. The "Replay motion" button above shows the fill-in. Everything stops under reduced motion.
