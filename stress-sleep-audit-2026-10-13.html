What Patterns would lose if the page went

Qi stagnation needs 5 clues for a full bar. This page can supply four of them on its own (hard to fall asleep, waking, stress, low mood), with no exam findings at all. That is why it matters for stress and insomnia patients.

If the page went outright: "stress", night sweats and throat lump would still arrive from Fang Bing Ren and Ask her taps, and "low mood" would still come from the men's, Change of life, PMS and cycle-log routes. "Hard to fall asleep" and "wakes in the night" would be gone for everyone, and "low mood" for women under 48 with no logged cycle days.
