What the counts say

At each formula count the jars jump up by hundreds of grams: +522, +260, +230, +230, +160, +142, +108 g in September, Wen Dan Tang 160 → 320 and Gui Zhi Fu Ling Wan 5 → 25 in October. A jar only goes up when a batch was made. So the batches are made, just not logged; the count puts the number right every 2–5 weeks, and between counts the app runs the jar down to 0.

The cost today: the Dashboard's Urgent stock shows 7 formulas as EMPTY and the Formula refill page says 6 are overdue, while the shelf has most of them. Those lists are noise until the next count.

Her answer (29 Oct): she does log a batch, by typing the jar's new Stock number in Inventory (and some on the phone). Typing the number changes the jar and nothing else: no batch goes in the log and the single herbs used are not taken off, so the herb numbers drift high and Running low misses them.

What I'd change, in order

  1. Log the batch where the jar is. The Dashboard row's Make ×1 sends her to the Formula refill bench; the row itself should take "I made 300 g" in one tap and a number (the grams field built today does the weighing when she wants it). And when she types a higher Stock number on a made-in-house jar, the app reads it as a batch: "Made 300 g? Yes · Just a count", logging it and taking the herbs off. A batch logged on the day keeps Urgent stock true.
  2. Make the formula count a 2-minute weekly grid. 24 jars, one row each, type the scale reading, done. It is what she already does every 2–5 weeks through the long Stocktake flow; as a tile on the Dashboard it becomes the formula ledger on purpose.
  3. Urgent stock only shouts about numbers it can trust. A formula jar not counted or made in 3 weeks shows a quiet "count me" instead of EMPTY, with the scale reading typed right there.
  4. To order in two steps. To order → Arrived, Copy for email counts as ordered; the On the way column folds away (never used in 3 months).
  5. Inventory as she keeps it. Her lean view (Name · Supplier · Bottle $ · Stock · Level) becomes the table; the row opens the rest (code, note, low line, status, cart). 300 taps a page become ~90.
  6. Rule fixes, one build: Dashboard names and Make / Order to 32px shapes; To order, Inventory and Suppliers names become plain text in rows that open; "🛒 Order all 3" and the week chips to 32px with the sprite icon.
  7. Admin fold: Price review (never used) folds into Suppliers as a button on each supplier's row; Stock statistics and Stock movements stay, they are read pages.
  8. Herb count nudge: a Dashboard line after 6 weeks ("Herbs last counted 27 Aug · Count the 16 running low"), since the herb numbers mostly hold.
